# Landmark checks against imagery and OpenStreetMap

November 2026. Each monument's model was rendered straight down (orthographic) and laid over Google zoom-19/20 and Esri satellite tiles together with the OpenStreetMap footprints, measured in the monument's own frame (metres; +z runs into the complex or out of the arch). Elevations were compared with published photos.

| Monument | Finding | Change |
|---|---|---|
| Rumi Darwaza | OSM way 45743889 is 77.6 m long and 15.6 m deep: the great arch stands proud of the west front line to z +4.4, and the wings and the back face reach z −11.2. The model's wings were 7 m deep and centred on the front line, and the end towers were 8.8 m across. A front-on drone photo shows each wing with a low ground arcade, an upper arcade of seven arches and a tall latticed parapet up to about 11.7 m, between slim three-tier octagonal towers about 6.5 m across. | Wings rebuilt to the mapped depth with those proportions; the east front moved back to z −11.2 with a vaulted passage between the two fronts; towers narrowed to the mapped 3.3 m radius. Motorbike rows moved clear of the east exit. |
| Picture Gallery | The footprint matches OSM way 1269878461 (main block, west and east wings). The roof on the imagery has two larger skylights behind three smaller ones, at park-frame (−95.9, −91.6), (−85.7, −91.6) and (−96.2 / −91.0 / −85.8, −81.6). The model had three skylights in a row and a large pyramid roof on the west wing; photos from the tank show the west wing flat-topped behind a latticed parapet, about 1.5 m above the main block. | Skylights set as on the imagery; pyramid removed; west wing lowered and given a latticed parapet; back wall moved to the mapped line at z −96. |
| Satkhanda | Google and Esri imagery both put the tower on OSM node "Satkhanda" (historic=monument) at (−211.1, −97.7), about 12 m north-west of the model's tower. The mosque beside it matched within 3 m. | Tower moved onto the mapped point; its garden and the mosque keep their places. |
| Bara Imambara and Asfi Mosque | The main hall, its front terrace and corner towers, the courtyard, the gardens, the second gateway and the mosque with its three domes all sit on the imagery and the OSM outlines (ways 212500740, 212500738, 212503399). | None. The Shahi Baoli is simplified to a tank; on the imagery it is a walled stepped court with a round well shaft at its east end. The mosque's small tank lies in the main courtyard rather than on the mosque platform. |
| Chota Imambara | The complex walls match OSM way 214212792 (±64.5 m, 150 m deep). The water channel, the two Taj-style tombs, the mosque (way 1269878459) and the main hall's dome sit on their imagery positions. | None. The hall's plinth is a few metres deeper than the four-point OSM outline (way 1269878456), but the dome lines up. |
| Clock Tower | The OSM footprint (way 214215447) is about 8.6 × 8.1 m. The 67 m tower leans by parallax on the imagery, so its base cannot be read from above. | Not checked in plan; elevation to be compared with photos. |

The overlay helper used for these checks is kept outside the repository; the measurements above are enough to repeat them.
